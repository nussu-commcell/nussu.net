NUS Campus Tours

Learn about our rich heritage, explore our tree-lined campus and immerse yourself in the dynamic student life of Singapore's flagship university. Whether virtually or in-person, NUS provides a plethora of opportunities to experience our vibrant campus and academic offerings firsthand.

Important Notice

Please note the following blackout dates for campus tours due to events involving high student participation and increased traffic. All student-led guided tours will not be available on the dates below.

  • 6 to 8 August 2026
  • 12 to 14 August 2026

Please also note the access restricted periods:

  • 3 August to 21 August 2026
  • 28 September to 9 October 2026

Access restrictions for Internal Shuttle Buses and Food Courts at UTown between 3 August 2026 and 21 August 2026, as well as 28 September 2026 and 9 October 2026.

Please note that during this period:

Internal Shuttle Buses (ISB) access will be restricted to only NUS students, staff and authorised persons from Mondays to Fridays from 8am to 2pm. Checks may be conducted for NUS-approved passes.

Access to UTown Food Courts including Fine Food and Flavours will be strictly restricted to only NUS students, staff and authorised persons from Mondays to Fridays from 11am to 2pm. Checks may be conducted for NUS-approved passes at the entrances of these food courts.

Visitors are only allowed to visit the campus with the accompaniment of a licensed student tour guide. Tours not accompanied by licensed student tour guides (Student Ambassador or Student Docents) are prohibited.

Student Ambassador-led tours are currently available for booking. For more information, please visit here. You may wish to book a tour with a Student Ambassador or a Student Docent if you want to visit the campus during this period.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Please avoid interfering with ongoing student activities and refrain from taking photos or videos of students without their permission.
  • Be mindful of oncoming traffic. Do not obstruct driveways, roads, or pedestrian crossings, and refrain from standing or posing in these areas for photo taking.
